pixix desktop: JWM, st, xedit, xcalc, xclock, xload, xeyes and the
Xfbdev X server, every one compiled by pxx.

  Programs:   click the desktop, or Menu at the bottom left
  Windows:    drag the title bar; the buttons at its right minimise,
              maximise and close
  Log out:    Menu > Log out (back to the console)
